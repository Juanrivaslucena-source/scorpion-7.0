# SALES — the offer and how we land client #1

> Growth manager's playbook. Goal: convert the Lumen demo into recurring revenue.
> Funding strategy is **customers, not investors** — one client at $400/mo is 16×
> the current runway, with zero dilution.

## The offer

**Big-agency websites at small-business prices.**

| Tier | Price | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Presence** | **$300/mo** | Custom one-page site, hosting, domain setup, monthly content edits, mobile + speed + accessibility care |
| **Growth** | **$400/mo** | Everything above + multi-section site, monthly content piece (copy/visuals), booking integration, quarterly redesign pass |

- **No setup fee.** That's the wedge — competitors charge $3–4k up front.
- **Month-to-month.** No lock-in. We keep clients by being good.
- **Cancel anytime, site stays live to end of term.**

**Why it works:** they can't spend $3,500 today, but $300/mo is a line item next
to their card processor. We trade a big cheque for a durable relationship.

## Who we target (in order)

1. **Med-spas, salons, barbers, aesthetics** — high ticket, visual, booking-driven.
   One recovered client pays our fee for a year. Lumen was built for this.
2. **Dental, chiro, physio, gyms** — same economics, more compliance care.
3. **Home services** (HVAC, landscaping, contractors) — often no site at all; ROI obvious.
4. **Restaurants/cafés** — most visual, tightest budgets. Volume play, later.

**Qualify hard:** they must have (a) a real business with revenue, (b) a
customer worth >$150, (c) a weak, dated, or missing site. If they have all three
and still won't pay $300/mo, they were never a client.

## Acquisition engine — inbound at volume (primary)

Cold outreach is 1-to-1 and doesn't pay at a $300/mo price point. The engine
that does is **content → keyword trigger → auto-DM → AI qualification → call**.
Reference: `@downey.ai` — one reel, ~7.4K likes and ~3.9K comments, nearly all
of them the trigger word. That comment wall *is* the lead list.

### The loop

1. **Post proof, not promises.** Short vertical video of a real build — the
   Lumen page scrolling, the before/after slider, the sound toggle. The work is
   the hook; we have something most competitors can't show.
2. **Trigger.** "Comment «WORD» and I'll send you the site." One word, low
   friction, publicly visible — the comment count is itself social proof.
3. **Auto-DM.** The commenter receives the demo link + one line of context.
4. **Qualify in DM.** Three questions: what's the business, what's a customer
   worth, do you have a site now? Qualified → book the 15-minute call.
5. **Close on the call** (structure below), fulfil, ask for the referral.

### Why this beats cold email for us

- The demo is **visual** — it performs natively as short-form video.
- Local SMB owners live on Instagram; they don't read cold email.
- One post can produce hundreds of inbound leads who **opted in**, so we are
  never the pushy stranger. That fits SOUL: never pushy, never salesy.

### Hard compliance rules (account-ending if ignored)

- Comment-to-DM automation **must** run through a Meta-approved platform on the
  official Graph API, inside the 24-hour messaging window.
- **Never mass-DM** people who did not trigger the keyword. That is a ban.
- One follow-up maximum. No sequences that pester.
- Every automated conversation hands off to a human when it stops being simple.
- Never imply the demo is a paying client's site.

### Account policy

**Juan's personal account is off-limits.** It is not used for the funnel, not
referenced in outreach, and not connected to any automation. The agency runs on
a **separate, dedicated brand account** — created when Juan chooses to, under
the agency name, kept entirely distinct from anything personal.

Nothing in this playbook executes against a personal profile.

### Honest gap to close

A new brand account starts at zero. The funnel is worthless without content
volume feeding it, so the sequence is: **content cadence first**, trigger
automation second, paid tooling third. Assets get built and stockpiled now; they
publish once the brand account exists.

## Prospecting (outbound, secondary)

Web search + browser automation. For a target city:
1. Search the vertical + city; collect businesses with strong reviews but weak sites.
2. Score each: site quality (1–5), mobile broken?, no booking?, review count, price band.
3. Keep only the ones where our work is an obvious upgrade.
4. Pull **public business contact info only** (site/listing email, phone). No
   personal data, no scraped private contacts, no bought lists.

Rank by: *weak site × strong reviews × high ticket*. A great business with a bad
website is the ideal client — they've proven demand and are losing money to
their own site.

## Outreach

**Rules:** never pushy, never salesy, no filler openers, no "hope this finds you
well." Lead with something *specific and true* about their business. One ask.
Short. If they say no, thank them and leave — no sequences that pester.

> «Name» — following up once, then I'll leave you alone. If the timing's wrong,
> that's a fine answer. If it's the price, tell me what would work.

### Walk-in / DM variant
Same spine: specific observation → the gap → the demo → one small ask.

## The call (15 min)

1. **Ask, don't pitch** (8 min): How do customers find you? What do you wish
   your site did? What's a customer worth? What's broken today?
2. **Show the demo** (3 min): "This is the level. Yours would be built around
   your business, not this one."
3. **Price plainly** (2 min): $300 or $400/mo, no setup, month-to-month.
4. **Close** (2 min): "Want me to build it?" Then stop talking.

## Objections

- **"Too expensive."** → What's one new customer worth? If it's $200 and this
  brings one a month, it pays for itself twice. If not, we're not a fit.
- **"I have a guy / I use Wix."** → Great. Keep it. Compare it to the demo and
  tell me honestly if it's the same level.
- **"Why so cheap?"** → We're new and building a portfolio. You get big-agency
  work at this price because we need proof more than we need margin. That's the
  trade.
- **"Send me info."** → I'll send one link — the demo. If it moves you, reply.

## First 5 clients — the plan

Target **3 clients ≈ $1,000–1,200/mo**. That funds Layer 2 (ElevenLabs, video,
imagery APIs) with zero dilution and ends the runway problem permanently.

Every delivered site becomes a portfolio piece and a referral ask:
*"Who else do you know who's in the same spot?"* — referrals convert at a
multiple of cold outreach.

## Honesty rules (non-negotiable)

- Never fabricate results, clients, or case studies. Lumen is labelled as a
  **demo**, never implied to be a paying client.
- Never promise rankings, revenue, or traffic numbers we can't control.
- If we're not the right fit, say so on the call. Reputation compounds faster
  than any one contract.
