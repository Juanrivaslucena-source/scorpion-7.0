## Positioning

**One line:** Big-agency websites for small businesses — without the big-agency price.

**Proof-first, not promise-first.** We show the work scrolling on screen and let
people judge it. No hype, no "10x your revenue," no guru posturing. The
differentiator is that we can *show* craft most competitors can't.

## Content pillars

1. **The work** (50%) — builds scrolling, hover states, before/after slider,
   sound toggle, loader. Silent-readable, caption-driven.
2. **The teardown** (25%) — a common small-business site problem and the fix.
   Useful even to people who never hire us. This is what earns saves and shares.
3. **The build-in-public** (15%) — how the studio actually works, the standard we
   hold, the honest constraints.
4. **The offer** (10%) — pricing stated plainly. Rare, so it lands.

Never post: fake results, invented clients, guru advice, engagement bait
unrelated to the work, or anything implying the demo is a paying client's site.

## Format rules

- **Vertical 9:16**, 7–20 seconds for work clips.
- **Hook in the first 1.5s** — motion on screen before any text.
- **Captions always on.** Most viewers are muted.
- **One idea per post.** No carousels of five ideas.
- **End on the work, not on a logo.** Let the last frame be the craft.

## Cadence

Start **3 posts/week**, sustainable beats heroic. Same two days each week plus
one opportunistic. Consistency is the entire algorithm strategy.

## Growth mechanics — in order, no shortcuts

1. **Volume of proof** — nothing works before this.
2. **Comment-to-DM trigger** — only once posts reach real reach. Must run on a
   Meta-approved platform via the official Graph API, inside the 24-hour window.
   Never DM anyone who didn't trigger it.
3. **Local targeting** — geo-tag the city, engage genuinely with local business
   accounts. No follow/unfollow games, no pods, no bought followers.
