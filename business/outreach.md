# Outreach Scripts

## The one rule

**Attach a finished clip cut from their own video.** Not a pitch, not an offer to make one — a
thing that already exists.

This is the entire strategy. A generic "I do video editing, are you interested?" converts at
roughly zero. A clip of *their* content, already done, converts because it removes every
objection at once: they can see the quality, they know you understand their show, and there's
nothing to imagine.

Yes, it means working before you're paid. That's the price of having no reputation, and it's the
cheapest price available. Budget ~20 minutes per prospect.

## Rules that keep you out of spam folders

- **One clip, one message.** No follow-up chains of 5. One polite follow-up after 4 days, then
  drop it forever.
- **Never mass-send.** If the message could be sent to anyone, it converts like it was.
- **No fake flattery.** "Love your content!" reads as a bot. Reference one specific moment.
- **Don't attach files to cold email** — it trips spam filters. Link to the clip (unlisted
  YouTube, Dropbox, Drive) or your portfolio page.
- **Don't claim results you can't back.** No "this will 10x your views." You don't know that, and
  the people you're pitching have heard it a thousand times from people who also didn't know it.

## Script 1 — Podcaster / YouTuber (email)

> **Subject:** cut a clip from ep. [NUMBER]
>
> Hi [NAME],
>
> I was listening to episode [NUMBER] — the part where you talked about [SPECIFIC THING, ~10
> words] stuck with me, so I cut it into a vertical clip with captions and a hook.
>
> It's here, yours to post whether or not we ever talk again: [LINK]
>
> I noticed you're publishing long-form weekly but nothing on Shorts or TikTok. If you want that
> covered, I do 5 clips per episode for $250, 3-day turnaround. More examples: [PORTFOLIO LINK]
>
> Either way, hope the clip's useful.
>
> [YOUR NAME]

**Why it works:** the giveaway is unconditional, the gap you noticed is specific and true, and
the price is stated. No "let's hop on a call to discuss your needs."

## Script 2 — Instagram / X / TikTok DM

Short. DMs get skimmed.

> [LINK]
>
> You're posting long-form but nothing short-form. I do 5 clips/episode for $250 if that's
> useful. Portfolio: [LINK]

## Script 3 — Coach / consultant / course seller

These buyers care about leads, not views. Adjust the frame.

> **Subject:** clip from your [WEBINAR / YOUTUBE / PODCAST]
>
> Hi [NAME],
>
> You explain [SPECIFIC CONCEPT] better in 40 seconds than most people manage in an hour, so I
> pulled that section into a vertical clip with captions: [LINK]
>
> Yours to use regardless. You've got a back catalogue of long videos and nothing short-form
> pointing back at them — that's usually the cheapest lead source a coach has sitting unused.
>
> 5 clips per video, $250, 3-day turnaround. Samples: [PORTFOLIO LINK]
>
> [YOUR NAME]

## Script 4 — Local business with a dead TikTok

Lower budgets. Lead with the written work too, since they rarely have anyone writing.

> Hi [NAME],
>
> Saw [BUSINESS] posted a few TikToks last year and then stopped — that's normal, it's a lot of
> work. I took your [SPECIFIC VIDEO/PHOTO] and made one vertical clip to show what a consistent
> version looks like: [LINK]
>
> Yours either way. If you want a month of it, I do 8 posts with captions written for $400.
>
> [YOUR NAME]

---

## Script 5 — The follow-up (send once, 4 days later)

> Hi [NAME] — just making sure the clip reached you: [LINK]. No pitch, it's yours regardless.
> If short-form isn't a priority right now, no problem at all, I won't follow up again.

That last sentence is not weakness. It gets replies, and it keeps you off block lists.

## When someone replies

**"How much again?"** → Restate the package, one sentence, then ask for the video link. Don't
re-sell. They're already sold.

**"Can you do a sample first?"** → You already did — that was the clip. Point back to it. Free
samples beyond that is how you spend a week working for nothing.

**"That's too expensive."** → "I get it. The 3-clip version is $150 if that's a better fit." Do
not go below that. Someone who won't pay $150 will be your most demanding client.

**"Can you do it cheaper if I send lots of work?"** → "Once we've done three episodes I'll do a
monthly rate at $1,200 for 4 episodes." Volume discounts come *after* proof, never before.

## Tracking

Keep a simple sheet: name, link, date sent, clip link, replied?, outcome. At 40–60 contacts a day
you *will* lose track and double-message someone, which is the one mistake that reliably makes
you look like a bot.
