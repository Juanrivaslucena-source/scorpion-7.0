# Platform Listing Copy

## The principle: list narrow, not broad

"Video editing" puts you against 40,000 sellers with 500 reviews each. You lose.

"Podcast → 5 TikTok clips" puts you against a few hundred, and matches what buyers actually type.
Narrow listings rank faster, convert better, and let you charge more because you read as a
specialist rather than a generalist who'll take anything.

Make **several narrow gigs**, not one broad one.

---

## Fiverr

Fiverr is the best starting platform: no portfolio gate, and buyers search *for you* rather than
you pitching them. Note funds are held **14 days** after order completion before withdrawal.

### Gig 1 — clips

**Title:** `I will turn your podcast into 5 viral ready TikTok reels and shorts`

**Category:** Video & Animation → Video Editing
**Search tags:** `podcast clips`, `short form video`, `reels editing`, `tiktok editor`, `captions`

**Description:**

> Your episode already has the good parts. They're just buried in 90 minutes nobody scrolls to.
>
> Send me your episode and I'll find the moments that stand alone, cut them vertical, write a
> hook for each one, and burn in captions. You get files ready to post — no extra work on your
> end.
>
> **What you get**
> - 5 vertical clips (9:16), 30–60 seconds each
> - Written hook on every clip
> - Burned-in captions, styled to match your brand
> - 1 round of revisions
>
> **What I need from you**
> - A link to the episode (YouTube, Drive, Dropbox — anything)
> - Your logo or brand colours, if you have them
>
> I write as well as edit, which matters more than it sounds: the hook is what decides whether
> the clip gets watched, and most editors treat it as an afterthought.

**Packages:** Basic $150 / 3 clips / 3 days · Standard $250 / 5 clips / 3 days ·
Premium $450 / 10 clips + platform captions / 5 days

### Gig 2 — writing

**Title:** `I will write clear blog posts and newsletters that do not sound AI generated`

**Search tags:** `blog writing`, `newsletter`, `seo article`, `copywriting`, `editing`

**Description:**

> Plain, specific writing that sounds like a person — because a person wrote it.
>
> I'll take your topic, outline, or half-finished draft and turn it into something publishable.
> No filler paragraphs restating the title, no "in today's fast-paced world."
>
> **What you get:** researched draft, your voice, one revision round, delivered in Google Docs
> or Markdown.
>
> Tell me the audience and what you want them to do after reading. That's usually all I need.

**Packages:** Basic $60 / 800 words · Standard $120 / 1,500 words · Premium $250 / 3,000 words +
outline

## Upwork

Widest pool of real client work. Budget for Connects and expect an early grind before proposals
land.

**Title:** `Short-Form Video Editor & Writer — Podcast/YouTube Repurposing`

**Overview (first two lines matter most — everything after is truncated in search):**

> I turn long videos into short clips people actually finish. Podcasts and YouTube episodes cut
> into vertical clips with written hooks and burned-in captions.
>
> Most editors can cut but write weak hooks. Most writers can't edit. I do both, which is why my
> clips hold attention past the three-second mark where most short-form dies.
>
> **Services:** podcast/YouTube repurposing · TikTok, Reels & Shorts · captions and subtitles ·
> blog posts, newsletters and SEO articles
>
> **Rate:** $30/hr, or fixed price — $250 for 5 clips from one episode, 3-day turnaround.
>
> Send me an episode link and I'll tell you honestly which moments are worth clipping before you
> commit to anything.

### Proposal template

Never reuse a generic proposal. Open with something only someone who read the posting could say.

> Hi [NAME],
>
> [ONE SENTENCE PROVING YOU READ THE POSTING — reference their show, their niche, the specific
> problem they described.]
>
> I've cut a clip from one of your episodes already so you can judge the work rather than take my
> word for it: [LINK]
>
> For what you described — [RESTATE THEIR ASK IN YOUR OWN WORDS] — I'd do [DELIVERABLE] for
> [PRICE], turned around in [DAYS].
>
> Portfolio: [LINK]
>
> [YOUR NAME]

That second line — a clip already cut — is worth more than every other word in the proposal.

## Contra / Hubstaff Talent

Free, no platform fees, good for building a public portfolio. Low traffic, so treat these as
places your portfolio link lives, not as a source of inbound work. Set them up once, in 20
minutes, and don't spend more time there.

Reuse the Upwork overview verbatim.

---

## Textbroker

Writing work you can start immediately with no portfolio and no client interaction. A rating test
sets your level; rates run roughly **$0.01–0.05/word**, paid weekly.

Be clear-eyed: at 3 stars, a 1,000-word article pays around $20. This is not the business — it's
paid practice that fills dead hours between edit jobs and produces samples you're allowed to
claim as your own work. Do it when the pipeline is empty, not instead of outreach.

---

## Setup order

1. Fiverr gigs (highest inbound potential, slowest to rank — start the clock today)
2. Upwork profile + 3 proposals
3. Contra / Hubstaff (20 minutes, then forget them)
4. Textbroker (only if you have idle hours)

Then stop optimising profiles and go do outreach. Platform ranking takes weeks no matter what you
do; outreach works today. Tweaking gig copy is the most comfortable way to avoid the actual job.
